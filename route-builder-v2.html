How to edit

1. Select a route. All controls become available when a route with geometry is loaded.

2. Edit Vertices. Click actual geometry points to multi-select them. Drag any selected/individual point to move it. Shift + drag a box across points to select many. Midpoint dots insert a point exactly into the segment.

3. Delete Selected Vertices. Removes all selected geometry points together. The editor will not let you delete the route down to fewer than two points.

4. Select Road Section. Click anywhere on the route where the bad section starts, then anywhere on the route where it ends. You do not need to hit an existing vertex.

5. Delete Section. Removes that exact section and creates a real gap. No line is drawn between the two remaining sides.

6. Draw Replacement. After a gap exists, click along the correct road. The replacement is drawn between the exact gap endpoints. Join Replacement restores one continuous route.

7. Simplify Geometry. Reduces unnecessary geometry points while preserving the overall shape. This is useful when OSRM created hundreds of nearly redundant points.

8. Show OSRM Alternative. Displays an OSRM proposal without overwriting your current geometry.

9. Rebuild with OSRM. This will replace your current manual geometry using the saved route waypoints. A confirmation is required.

10. Save Draft / Publish. Save Draft stores a recoverable local draft on this browser. Publish writes the continuous geometry to Supabase. Gapped routes cannot be published.

For the Philipsburg → Marigot correction, delete the unwanted OSRM section first. Then draw the Union Road section and join it.

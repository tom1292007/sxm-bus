Manual section correction
1. Click “Manual Section Edit”.
Click the existing blue route where the unwanted section STARTS.
2. Click the blue route where the unwanted section ENDS.

3. Click several points on the correct road.
Put 3–10 points along the road you actually want the bus to use.
4. Click “Apply Manual Section”.
OSRM will route only through your replacement points, and the old section will be replaced.

The corrected geometry is kept for saving. Do not click “Build Whole Route with OSRM” afterward unless you intentionally want to rebuild the whole route again.

Simple route editing

Build Whole Route: use this only when you want OSRM to calculate the entire route.

Add / Move Points: click the map to add a waypoint. New points are inserted in route order. Drag a numbered point to move it. Click a numbered point to delete it.

Cut Section: click the blue route at the beginning of the unwanted section, then click it again at the end. The section turns red.

Draw Replacement: click several points on the correct street. Your clicks are snapped to the nearest drivable road.

Apply Replacement: removes only the red section and puts your new replacement section in its place. The rest of the route stays unchanged.

Save Draft: stores your work in this browser without changing the live route in Supabase.

Publish: writes the current route geometry and points to Supabase.

Reverse Direction: reverses the current geometry and waypoint sequence. Review the endpoints before publishing.

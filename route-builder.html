Simple route correction
1. Select a route and make sure the blue route is visible.
2. Click Edit / Cut Route.
3. Click the blue route where the unwanted section starts. A red marker appears.
4. Click the blue route where the unwanted section ends. An orange marker appears.
5. Click Draw Replacement.
6. Click several points along the correct road. Purple points show your replacement.
7. Click Apply Replacement.
8. Check the blue route. Repeat if needed.
9. Click Save Route.

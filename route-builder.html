How to edit your route

Edit Route Vertices: shows the actual OSRM geometry points. Drag a point to move the road. Click points to select multiple vertices (selected points turn red), then use Delete Selected Vertices to remove them all at once.

Add Vertex: click anywhere along/near the route to insert a new geometry point. This changes the line directly — no OSRM rebuild is required.

Select Road Section: click the route where the unwanted section starts, then click where it ends.

Delete Section: deletes only that portion. The route is intentionally left with a visible gap. You can save that as a draft.

Draw Replacement: optional. Click several points along the correct road. The points are snapped to the nearest drivable road.

Join Replacement: inserts the replacement and closes the gap.

Build / Rebuild with OSRM: recalculates the entire route. Do not use this after making a manual correction unless you want OSRM to recalculate everything again.

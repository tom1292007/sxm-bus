How to edit Turn on Edit Points, then click on the correct road to add waypoints. Drag any existing blue point to move it. Click an existing point to delete it.

Put several points along a road when OSRM keeps choosing the wrong street. For example, place 3–5 points along Union Road to force the route through that corridor.

Then click Build with OSRM. Check the route. Only click Save Route when it is correct.

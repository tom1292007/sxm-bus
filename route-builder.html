Delete an unwanted OSRM section

1. Select Section — click the route where the unwanted section starts, then click where it ends.

2. Delete Selected — removes that section immediately. You do NOT need to create a replacement.

The route will remain with a visible gap. This is saved only as a local draft until you repair the gap.

3. Draw Replacement — optional. Use this only when you want to put a new road section into the gap.

4. Join Replacement — inserts your replacement points and closes the gap.

5. Publish — only allowed when the route is continuous.

Add / Move Points — click to add road points, drag to move them, click a numbered point to delete it.

Build Whole Route with OSRM — recalculates the entire route, so use this only deliberately.

Save Draft lets you keep a gapped route without changing the live route.
